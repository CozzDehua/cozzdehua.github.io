02 / RESEARCH

Research interests

Exploring

Secure and efficient execution environments for AI agents

My interests lie at the intersection of operating systems, systems security and AI agent infrastructure. I am exploring how to make agent tool execution lightweight, controllable and observable on existing Linux systems.

Lightweight sandboxing & isolation

Understanding container and sandbox isolation boundaries, and exploring Linux namespaces and permission controls to constrain agent tools' access to processes, files and networks.

Tool-level resource control & observability

Connecting tool calls to underlying processes, and exploring mechanisms such as cgroups and eBPF for resource monitoring and control, with a focus on CPU, memory, I/O and contention between concurrent tasks.

Sandbox pooling & execution lifecycles

Exploring pre-creation, reuse and reclamation of execution environments to understand startup latency and resource overhead, while ensuring task cleanup does not leave behind files, background processes or sensitive state.

  • Linux
  • Systems security
  • AI agents
  • Containers & sandboxes
  • Resource management
